Our Story

Yom Business was founded in 2020 E.C. with a vision to revolutionize how business information is accessed in Ethiopia. We recognized the need for a centralized, reliable platform where traders, investors, and consumers could find accurate, timely market data.

Today, we serve over 10,000 daily users across 50+ Ethiopian cities, providing real-time commodity prices, exchange rates, job listings, and a vibrant marketplace. Our commitment to accuracy and reliability has made us a trusted name in the Ethiopian business community.
