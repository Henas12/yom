Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I post an ad on Yom Business?

To post an ad, first create a free account. Then go to the Marketplace section and click "Post Listing". Fill in the details about your item and submit. Basic listings are free!

How often are prices updated?

Commodity prices are updated twice daily - at 8:00 AM and 3:00 PM. Exchange rates are updated in real-time from bank sources.

Is posting jobs free?

Basic job postings are free for small businesses. Premium listings with featured placement are available for a small fee.

What items are not allowed on the marketplace?

Illegal items, counterfeit products, drugs, weapons, and any items prohibited by Ethiopian law are not allowed. We also prohibit hateful or discriminatory content.
